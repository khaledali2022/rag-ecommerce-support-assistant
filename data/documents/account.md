# Account

## How do I create an account?
Click "Sign Up", provide your email address and a password, and verify your email through the confirmation link we send you.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. The link expires after 24 hours.

## Can I have multiple shipping addresses saved?
Yes, you can save multiple addresses under "Address Book" in your account settings and choose one at checkout.

## How do I delete my account?
Go to Account Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period, during which you can cancel the request by logging back in.

## Can I merge two accounts?
Account merging is not supported automatically. Contact support with both account emails and we can manually transfer order history to a single account.

## How do I update my email address?
Go to Account Settings > Profile, enter the new email, and confirm it via the verification link sent to the new address before the change takes effect.
