# Shipping

## What shipping methods are available?
We offer three shipping methods: Standard (5-7 business days), Express (2-3 business days), and Same-Day (available only in select metro areas, order must be placed before 12:00 PM local time).

## How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Below that threshold, Standard shipping costs $4.99. Express shipping costs $12.99 regardless of order value. Same-Day delivery costs $19.99.

## Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes, which are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the item price or shipping cost.

## How do I track my shipment?
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email and SMS. You can also find the tracking link under "My Orders" in your account.

## What happens if my package is lost in transit?
If tracking shows no movement for more than 7 business days (14 for international orders), contact support. We will open an investigation with the courier and, if the package is confirmed lost, issue a replacement or full refund.

## Can I change the delivery address after the order has shipped?
Once an order has shipped, the delivery address cannot be changed through our system. You may be able to redirect the package directly with the courier using the tracking link, subject to the courier policies.
