# Products

## How do I know if an item is in stock?
The product page shows real-time stock status: "In Stock", "Low Stock" (fewer than 5 units left), or "Out of Stock". Out-of-stock items can be tracked with a "Notify Me" button.

## Do product images accurately represent the item?
We aim for accurate representation, but slight color variation can occur due to display settings. Detailed specifications and size charts are provided on each product page to reduce mismatches.

## How do I find the right size?
Each clothing and footwear product page includes a size chart with measurements. We recommend measuring a similar item you already own and comparing it to the chart.

## Are your electronics covered by warranty?
Electronics come with the manufacturer standard warranty, typically 12 months, details of which are listed on the product page. Extended warranty plans can be added at checkout for eligible items.

## Can I leave a review for a product I purchased?
Yes, once your order is marked "Delivered", you will receive an email invite to leave a rating and written review, which also appears under "My Orders".

## What if the product I received does not match the description?
This qualifies as a "wrong item" return, which is free to return and includes a full refund of both the item price and original shipping cost.
