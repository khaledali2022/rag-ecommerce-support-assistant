# Returns

## What is your return policy?
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unused, in their original packaging, and include all tags and accessories.

## Which items cannot be returned?
Final sale items, personal care products, perishable goods, and any item marked "Non-Returnable" on the product page cannot be returned.

## How do I start a return?
Go to "My Orders", select the item you want to return, choose a reason, and print the prepaid return label. Drop the package at any authorized carrier location within 14 days of generating the label.

## Do I have to pay for return shipping?
Return shipping is free if the return is due to a defect, wrong item shipped, or damage during transit. For returns due to a change of mind, a $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## How long does a return take to process once received?
Once the returned item arrives at our warehouse, inspection takes 3-5 business days. You will receive an email confirming whether the return was accepted.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Yes, for clothing and footwear you can request a size or color exchange instead of a refund, subject to availability of the requested variant.
