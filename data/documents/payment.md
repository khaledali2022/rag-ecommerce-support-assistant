# Payment

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and in some regions, cash on delivery.

## Is it safe to save my card on file?
Yes. Card details are tokenized and stored by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor; our systems never store your full card number.

## Why was my payment declined?
Common reasons include insufficient funds, incorrect card details, the card issuer blocking the transaction as a security precaution, or the billing address not matching the card on file.

## Can I pay in installments?
Installment plans (Pay in 4) are available at checkout for orders over $35 in supported regions, through our third-party installment partner.

## Will I be charged immediately when I place an order?
For card payments, an authorization hold is placed immediately and the charge is captured when the order moves to "Processing". For cash on delivery, no charge occurs until delivery.

## How do I get an invoice for my order?
An invoice is automatically emailed after your order is placed, and is also downloadable as a PDF from the order detail page.
