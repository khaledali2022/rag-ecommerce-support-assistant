# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you have a physical store?
We are an online-only retailer and do not currently operate physical retail locations.

## How can I contact customer support?
You can reach support through live chat (available 24/7), email at support@example.com, or by phone during business hours (9 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday).

## Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, gift wrapping is available as a paid add-on at checkout for $3.99 per item, and includes an optional personalized message card.

## Are prices inclusive of tax?
Displayed prices exclude tax in most regions; applicable sales tax is calculated and shown at checkout based on your shipping address.

## Do you price-match competitors?
We do not offer price matching, but we do run seasonal sales and allow you to apply one active promo code per order.

## How do I unsubscribe from marketing emails?
Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any marketing email, or turn off email notifications in Account Settings > Notifications. Transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates) cannot be disabled.
