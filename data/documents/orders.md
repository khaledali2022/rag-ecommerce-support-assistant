# Orders

## How do I place an order?
Browse the product catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You will need to provide a shipping address and a valid payment method to complete the order.

## Can I change my order after placing it?
Orders can be modified within 1 hour of placement, as long as the order has not yet entered the "Processing" stage. After that, the order is locked and cannot be changed. Contact support immediately if you need to make a change.

## How do I check my order status?
Log into your account and go to "My Orders". Each order shows one of the following statuses: Pending, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered, or Cancelled.

## What do the order statuses mean?
- Pending: payment is being verified.
- Processing: the order is being prepared in the warehouse.
- Shipped: the order has left the warehouse and is with the courier.
- Out for Delivery: the courier is on the way to the delivery address.
- Delivered: the order has been handed to the customer.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled by the customer or by support.

## Can I combine two separate orders into one shipment?
No. Orders placed separately are processed and shipped independently, even if placed on the same day, because they may enter the warehouse queue at different times.

## What is the maximum number of items per order?
There is no fixed maximum, but orders containing more than 50 line items may be flagged for manual review before processing.
