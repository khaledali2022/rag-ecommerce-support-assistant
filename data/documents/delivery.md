# Delivery

## What if I am not home when the courier arrives?
The courier will typically attempt delivery up to 3 times. After a failed attempt, the package is usually left at a nearby pickup point, and you will get a notification with pickup instructions.

## Can I schedule a specific delivery time?
Standard and Express shipping do not support scheduled time slots. Same-Day delivery, where available, offers a 2-hour delivery window selectable at checkout.

## What should I do if my package arrives damaged?
Take photos of the damaged package and item, then report it through "My Orders" within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or refund without requiring you to return the damaged item.

## What if the tracking says Delivered but I never received the package?
First check with neighbors and any building reception, and look around the delivery location, since couriers sometimes leave packages in a safe spot. If you still cannot find it after 24 hours, contact support to open a missing-package investigation.

## Do you deliver to PO boxes?
Standard shipping can deliver to PO boxes in most regions. Express and Same-Day delivery require a physical street address.
