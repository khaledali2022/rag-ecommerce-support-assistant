# Refunds

## When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-10 business days after the returned item is inspected and accepted. Bank processing times may add a few extra days.

## Will I get a full refund?
You will receive a full refund of the item price if the return meets policy conditions. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defect, or damage).

## Can I get a refund without returning the item?
In some cases, such as a missing item from your order, or a clearly defective item that support has approved for disposal, a refund can be issued without requiring a physical return.

## Can I get store credit instead of a refund to my card?
Yes, you can choose store credit at checkout of the return flow. Store credit is issued instantly once the return is accepted, rather than waiting for card processing times.

## What if my refund has not arrived after 10 business days?
Contact support with your order number. We will check the refund status with our payment processor and escalate if the funds have not reached your account.
