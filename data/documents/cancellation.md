# Cancellation

## Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled for free as long as they are still in "Pending" or "Processing" status. Once an order reaches "Shipped" status, it can no longer be cancelled.

## How do I cancel an order?
Go to "My Orders", select the order, and click "Cancel Order". If the cancel option is not available, the order has already moved past the cancellable stage.

## Will I be charged if I cancel?
No. If the order is successfully cancelled before shipping, any authorization hold on your card is released and no charge is captured.

## What if the cancel button is greyed out but I still want to cancel?
This means the order has already entered a stage where automatic cancellation is not possible. Contact support immediately; if the item has not yet physically left the warehouse, an agent may still be able to intercept it.

## Can I cancel just one item from a multi-item order?
Yes, as long as that specific item has not yet started processing. Partial cancellation adjusts the total charge and refunds the cancelled item amount.
